description: Write every field so a busy stakeholder gets it in one glance. Use for all text people will read on screen - questions, reasons, options, briefs, answers.

People skim. Every extra word costs attention they won't give. Write the shortest text that still decides something.

## Word budgets (hard limits)

| Text | Max |
|---|---|
| Title | 5 words |
| Understanding, goal | 15 words, one sentence |
| Question | 12 words, ends with "?" |
| Why (reason for a question) | 10 words |
| Answer option, conflict option | 6 words |
| Trade-off | 12 words |
| List item (scope, criteria, known facts, open issues, decisions) | 10 words |
| Subtask detail | 12 words |
| Source of a decision | "Q3 Anna", "W1 Ben", "D1 owner" - just the reference |
| Answer to a worker | 2 sentences |

For Chinese, count about 2 characters per English word.

## Lists

- 3-5 items. Merge near-duplicates. Drop anything obvious.

## Style

- Lead with the point. No preamble ("This task is about...", "In order to...").
- Concrete nouns and numbers over adjectives ("max 2 h", not "reasonable duration").
- Never restate the question or the task back.
- No hedging, no filler, no "etc.", no parenthetical asides.
- Plain words. Write for someone reading on a phone between meetings.
